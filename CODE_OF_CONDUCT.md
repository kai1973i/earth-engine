# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in TerraScope to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming a mistake.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with care.
- Credit data sources, previous work and contributors.
- Respect data licenses and attribution requirements; never strip attribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or sexualized attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour in issues, reviews or discussions.
- Adding telemetry, trackers or data without attribution.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions and any project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, remove comments, close threads or ban contributors for
behaviour that violates this code. Reports can be sent privately to the
maintainers. Reports will be handled confidentially.
